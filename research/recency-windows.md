# Recency windows - how long a prior result counts as "already done"

Purpose: for each pathway test, the number of days a prior result stays relevant for a new cardiology referral, and what makes an older result stale regardless of age. Draft windows come from pathways/cardiology.json planning; no guideline sets a "valid_days" value for a referral, so every number below is a Lantern design choice unless a retest interval is cited. Verdicts refer to whether a source supports the proposed number.

## Checked against
- NICE NG106 Chronic heart failure in adults (UK, updated 3 Sep 2025), full text: 1.7.4 (renal function and electrolytes 1-2 weeks after starting/changing RAAS drugs; every 3-6 months once stable); 1.2.7 (treatment lowers NT-proBNP).
- 2025 AHA/ACC High Blood Pressure guideline (Circulation 2025;152:e114-e218), full text: 3.1.2 + Table 6 (basic labs, repeated at least annually); 3.1.4 (ABPM/HBPM for diagnosis and titration).
- ADA, "6. Glycemic Goals and Hypoglycemia: Standards of Care in Diabetes-2025", Diabetes Care 2025;48(Suppl 1) - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/11635034 , recommendation 6.2. (The 2026 edition of Section 6 changes the assessment recommendation to A1C and/or CGM metrics; not yet read.)
- AAFP, "Cholesterol Management: ACC/AHA Updates Guideline", Am Fam Physician 2019 (summary of the 2018 AHA/ACC cholesterol guideline, now superseded by the 2026 dyslipidemia guideline) - https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2019/0501/p589.html
- ACC, "Key Imaging Takeaways From the 2021 Chest Pain Guidelines", 2022 - https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/articles/2022/03/14/11/34/key-imaging-takeaways-from-the-2021-chest-pain-guidelines ; AAFP, "Chest Pain Evaluation: Updated Guidelines From the AHA/ACC", 2023 - https://www.aafp.org/afp/2023/0200/practice-guidelines-chest-pain-evaluation
- AHA newsroom, "ACC, AHA, HFSA Issue Heart Failure Guideline", 2022 - https://newsroom.heart.org/news/acc-aha-hfsa-issue-heart-failure-guideline?print=1
- ACC, "2020 ACC/AHA Heart Valve Disease Guideline: Key Perspectives, Part 1", 2020 - https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/ten-points-to-remember/2020/12/16/21/58/2020-ACC-AHA-VHD-GL-Pt-1-GL-VHD
- ACC, "2023 ... AFib Guideline" summaries (see afib.md).
- Not yet checked: KDIGO 2024 Figure 13 (monitoring frequency by GFR/albuminuria category); 2022 HF guideline recommendation text on repeat LVEF; 2020 VHD guideline surveillance-interval tables; ASE appropriate-use criteria; ATA/other TSH retest guidance; 2023 AF guideline monitoring section.

## Cross-cutting rule suggested by the sources
Where a source gives an interval it is tied to a change: renal function/electrolytes after RAAS changes (NG106 1.7.4), lipids after statin changes (2018 cholesterol via AAFP), A1c after treatment change (ADA 6.2), imaging after clinical change or inconclusive prior test (ACC imaging 2022; 2022 HF). A change in symptoms, medication or hospitalization after the result date should therefore override a fixed window; this is a design decision for cardiologist review.

## For cardiologist review
- Whether a single `valid_days` per test is acceptable or windows should depend on diabetes/CKD/HF status.
- ECG, NT-proBNP, echo and monitor windows have no source for a number; consider framing as "since last symptom/med change".
